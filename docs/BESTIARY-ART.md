# Bestiary — art direction

Visual descriptions for the eight combat creatures, for the portrait set
(`img/enemy/<id>.png`). Pulled from the in-game encounter prose so the art matches the
writing. **Style note (to stay cohesive with the first two portraits):** antique
**engraving / etching** — fine cross-hatched line, Doré/woodcut lineage, near-monochrome
(blue-black or sepia ink on aged paper). The established compositional motif is the
creature **filling a rough stone sewer-arch / pipe-mouth, heaving toward the viewer out of
the dark** — keep that framing and the set will read as one plate-series.

The metaphysic to keep in mind: *the filth of the earth swirls together into an evil greater
than the sum of its parts.* The sewer creatures are the mutagen at work; the hell creatures
are made things and true demons. Horror deepens with depth.

---

## `rat.png` — demon rat  *(sewer; the weakest enemy)*
A mutated vermin, **not** cute and **not** small: a rat the size of a dog, **hairless,
wet, and swollen wrong**, slick pinkish-grey skin stretched over a body that has fattened
on a hundred of its own drowned kin. **Too many eyes** (four, five — set wrong, too
interested), a naked worm-tail, overlong yellow incisors. Low to the ground, hunched,
mid-scurry. Frame it coming off a raft of dead rats in a grit-channel, or out of a
gnawed-open gearbox. The *first wrong thing* the player meets — vermin that the deep has
begun to edit.

## `shitgolem.png` — shit-golem / the sewage demon  *(sewer; the blunt-weak wall)*  ✅ ART DONE
*(You've drawn this one.)* The thesis made flesh: a thousand discarded harmless things —
bone, rag, matted hair, sludge — pooled and **stood up** into one slow enormous body, a man
twice your size with no real face, only the place a face would go. Eyeless, lumpen, sagging,
squatting over bones. "Far more than the sum of the muck that makes it."

## `crawler.png` — drain-crawler  *(deep hinge, L3)*
**Not a rat. Longer.** A blind, pale, eel-or-salamander thing **the colour of a root grown
in the dark and never finished** — wet translucent white, you can almost see what's under
the skin. **No eyes at all**, and a **mouth that opens far too wide and too eagerly** for a
head that has nothing to aim it with — it hunts by want, not sight. Many short scrabbling
limbs, a long boneless body folded into a tight crawlspace. The first thing past the hinge
that fits *no clean category* — show it nosing blindly out of a low pipe, jaw already gaping.

## `drowned.png` — the drowned  *(the between, L4)*
The drowned dead the flood keeps and **raises for a passing lamp**. A waterlogged human
corpse, **bloated and pruned and bloodless pale**, hair plastered, clothes rotted to the
body — **unfolding joint by waterlogged joint** up out of black still floodwater, **drowned
hands out** toward the light, **drowned mouth working around water instead of air** (water
spilling from it, not breath). Ecclesiastical staging: it rises off a flooded **kneeler** in
a sunken chapel/choir, others ranked behind it in the dark water. Grief, not malice — which
is worse.

## `grafted.png` — grafted thing  *(hell, L7 — the Belly of the Beast)*
Assembled body-horror: **a thing that should have been structure and became a creature
instead.** Stitched and woven from **every spare part to hand — too many joints and not
enough plan**, limbs that don't match, a torso half-**woven into a wall-loom of bone and
sinew** and then *kept*, dragging itself free of the weave it was meant to be. Its signature
is **wrong eyes — too many, borrowed, scattered across it where eyes shouldn't be.** Cronenberg
assemblage; pity and revulsion. Frame it tearing loose from the loom, trailing threads of
tissue.

## `gorger.png` — Gorger  *(hell, L8 — the Rendering Works; a tanky wall)*
The things that **grow on discarded flesh and now render it at scale** (the named demon
Tallow is one). **Fat, pale, soft, and tireless** — a great pale **grub-or-hog-bulk** of a
labourer, a huge featureless pale head that turns slow to *consider* you, small or no eyes,
**up to its elbows in the work**. Industrial, not feral: think a rendering-house worker made
of the rendered, maybe slung with an apron of cooled fat, a long skimming paddle to hand,
steam and tallow-light around it. Placid, enormous, unstoppable.

## `pigdemon.png` — pig-demon  *(hell, L7 den / L9 sulfur castle; the kidnapper's kind)*
The true demon at the bottom — **the answer to who took you.** A **massive upright boar**,
**wet pink hide the size of a manhole at the snout**, heavy tusks, bristled, and **two small
black eyes that have done this many, many times before** (the cruelty is the *boredom* in
them). Boars that work the belly hauling meat; vast pale sows in the sty; and the **lord of
the sulfur castle** crowned in canary-yellow brimstone crust. Regal and brutal — the
intro-kidnapper "the size of the thing that took you." For the L9 lord, push the scale and
the throne; for the common pig-demon, the working boar.
